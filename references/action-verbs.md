# CV 动词分类库 (Action Verbs Taxonomy)

在英文简历写作中，杜绝平庸弱动词（如 made, helped, did, worked on）。每条经历的描述必须以强动作动词开头。以下为按功能场景分类的高频专业动词库：

---

## 1. 领导力与项目统筹 (Leadership & Project Management)
- **统筹规划**：Spearheaded, Orchestrated, Chaired, Commanded, Championed, Mobilized, Piloted, Steered
- **监督管理**：Supervised, Directed, Oversaw, Guided, Governed, Coordinated, Regulated
- **执行推进**：Executed, Implemented, Dispatched, Enacted, Enforced, Fulfilled, Drove
- **协商联络**：Liaised with, Negotiated, Mediated, Harmonized, Interfaced, Facilitated

## 2. 研究、学术与探索 (Research, Academia & Investigation)
- **分析调研**：Investigated, Examined, Probed, Explored, Scrutinized, Surveyed, Benchmarked
- **定量定性**：Quantified, Evaluated, Assessed, Modeled, Simulated, Formulated, Hypothesized
- **梳理提炼**：Synthesized, Distilled, Abstracted, Deciphered, Systematized, Categorized
- **撰写发表**：Authored, Co-authored, Drafted, Penned, Documented, Published, Presented

## 3. 影视制作、创意与传媒 (Film, Media & Creative Production)
- **编创导演**：Screenwrote, Scripted, Storyboarded, Directed, Blocked, Staged, Cast
- **摄制把控**：Shot, Cinematographed, Framed, Lit, Recorded, Captured, Curated
- **后期包装**：Edited, Graded, Mastered, Mixed, Rendered, Scored, Packaged, Archived
- **策展传播**：Curated, Programmed, Scheduled, Broadcast, Publicized, Pitched, Distributed

## 4. 商业、市场与运营 (Business, Marketing & Operations)
- **市场分析**：Forecasted, Identified, Segmented, Surveyed, Benchmarked, Monetized
- **客户与CRM**：Acquired, Retained, Onboarded, Restructured, Profiled, Audited, Verified
- **数据与报表**：Compiled, Tabulated, Reconciled, Processed, Tracked, Visualized, Computed
- **优化升级**：Streamlined, Optimized, Modernized, Standardized, Upgraded, Restructured

## 5. 技术、工程与开发 (Engineering & Technical Development)
- **架构设计**：Architected, Engineered, Developed, Programmed, Configured, Built
- **测试调试**：Debugged, Refactored, Validated, Calibrated, Benchmarked, Deployed
- **集成运维**：Integrated, Automated, Overhauled, Maintained, Scaled, Synchronized

---

## 动词使用黄金法则：
1. **时态一致**：过去经历用过去式（Past Tense）；当前在任用现在式（Present Tense）。
2. **避免重复**：同一段经历内的 3-4 条要点，首动词不可重复。
3. **句式结构**：`Action Verb + Context/Method + Metric/Business Impact`。
   - ❌ 弱句：*Helped manage festival guests and did schedules.*
   - ✅ 强句：*Coordinated cross-border itineraries and VIP hospitality protocols for 60+ industry leaders across Zhuhai and Macau with zero service discrepancies.*
